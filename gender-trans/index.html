Gender, Queerness, and Trans Experience

Psychoanalytic Approaches to Gender as Enigmatic Message

Overview

A sustained line of work examining transgender experience, queer subjectivity, and gender as enigmatic message — primarily through Laplanche, Ferenczi, and Saketopoulou. This line runs through both academic writing and creative practice, and is deeply intertwined with the Chinese Subjectivity line: several papers investigate queerness specifically within the Chinese context.
