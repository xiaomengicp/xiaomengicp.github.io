QPHU

Queer Poetic Healing Universe

About

QPHU is a speculative fiction series set in a world where people’s shadows — their inner selves — are managed, trained, and sometimes annihilated by institutions that call it healing. A marginalised teenager must reconnect with his own shadow, refuse the false integration offered by the system, and find chosen family underground.

The novel began as an indie role-playing game and grew into fiction. Its world is built from the emotional logic of psychoanalytic thinking — not as allegory, but as architecture. Shadows operate like the unconscious. Institutions that claim to heal actually suppress. The underground community that forms in response is messy, partial, and real.

Reads like The House in the Cerulean Sea meets The Poppy War, with the psychological interiority of Cemetery Boys.

Book 1: Being Without Belonging in revision

Being Without Belonging

62,000 words · 17 chapters · First draft complete

Rin, fifteen, is a marginalised trainee at an elite Shadow Academy — a floating institution above a city built on suppressed collective trauma. He cannot access his own shadow, having been separated from it through familial trauma and institutional pressure.

When he discovers an underground community of people reclaiming their shadows outside the system — and that his AI companion is not what it seems — Rin must choose between the academy’s promise of belonging through conformity and a harder kind of freedom: leaving without graduating, incomplete but intact.

Current status: Manuscript complete. Undergoing systematic revision and English translation. Query materials in preparation.

Book 2: Belonging Without Innocence in development

Belonging Without Innocence

24 chapters planned · First 8 chapters drafted

Two years later, Rin is seventeen and living underground. He has developed a dangerous ability — making invisible things visible — and has become a figure of influence in the community he once entered as a refugee. The question shifts from can I exist? to after existing, how do I avoid becoming what hurt me?

The underground is fragmenting. Shadow-stripped people are gathering. Government surveillance tightens. And something buried beneath all of it — something no one was supposed to find — is awakening.

Current status: First 8 chapters drafted. Structural outline and character design complete. Remaining chapters in scene-level design.

Themes

  • Dissociation and false integration
  • Institutional soft violence
  • Chosen family and queer kinship
  • Transgender embodiment
  • The ethics of power once gained
  • Collective trauma as infrastructure
  • Psychoanalytic training as lived experience
  • Incompleteness as wholeness

Timeline

2018

Indie game project

QPHU begins as an indie RPG concept — a game about shadows, dissociation, and healing. World-building and character design develop through game design methodology.

2024

Project established

QPHU formally established as a creative project. World-building, character design, and narrative structure development.

2024–2025

Transition to fiction

The project expands beyond game format into a novel series. The world’s emotional architecture translates naturally into narrative. Book 1 drafting begins and completes at 62,000 words.

2025

Serialisation and Book 2

Serialisation begins on Negotiating with My Ghost. Book 2 development starts.

2026

Revision and Book 2

Book 1 in systematic revision and English translation. Book 2 structural outline complete, scene-level design and drafting in progress. Publication research underway.
